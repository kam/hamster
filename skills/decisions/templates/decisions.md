# Decisions

Standing decisions for this project. One row per decision; a changed decision gets a new row and the old row's Status becomes `superseded by DEC-<n>`, then moves to `docs/decisions/archive.md`. `decided` rows name the person who decided; anything inferred is `proposed`. Sources live git-excluded in `.claude/decisions/sources/` and are cited as `<path> sha:<12>`. Format and rules: hamster's `decisions` skill.
